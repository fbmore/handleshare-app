Wi-Fi

Stop spelling out your password

Put your network on your card and guests join by scanning a code — no reading characters aloud across the room. Made for offices, studios, rentals and cafés.

  • Standard Wi-Fi QR — works with any phone
  • Stays on your device and your private iCloud

Scan

Paper cards, digitised on the spot

Handed a paper business card? Point the camera at it and Handleshare reads the details and offers to save them to Contacts. Your scan history keeps everything you've collected in one place.

  • Reads names, numbers, emails and addresses
  • Scan history you can search later
