Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Email support@handleshare.com — we read every message.

Including your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened helps us help you faster.

Getting started

Open Handleshare and fill in your card: name, title, links, photo — whatever you want to hand people. Tap Share to show your QR code; anyone can scan it with their iPhone camera, no app required on their end.

Profiles and presets

Keep a separate profile for each part of your life (work, a side business, personal, events), each with its own photo, bio, links, files and QR code. Switch between them from the profile switcher.

A preset is a saved version of a profile for one situation, like "Investor meeting" or "Job search". It remembers which details, links, credentials and files go out. In the share sheet, switch on what you want and tap Save as preset (or New preset); next time, tap the preset to switch everything on in one go, then change anything you like for that one share. Touch and hold a preset to save the current choices to it, rename it or delete it. Items you add to a profile after saving a preset follow their own "include by default" setting until you save the preset again.

Free includes 2 profiles with up to 2 presets each; Pro removes both limits. For now, presets are stored only on the iPhone where you make them: they don't sync through iCloud to your other devices yet.

Sharing your Wi-Fi

Add your network name and password to your card, then show the Wi-Fi QR code. Guests scan it with their camera and join your network directly. Only share networks you're authorized to give access to.

Scanning a paper business card

Use the in-app scanner and point the camera at the card. Handleshare reads the details and offers to save them to your Contacts — it will ask for Contacts permission the first time.

The camera or scanner isn't working

  • Check that Handleshare has camera access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Handleshare.
  • Make sure there's enough light and the QR code or card fills the frame.

My cards aren't syncing across devices

Cards sync through your private iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled for Handleshare (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud). Sync happens in the background shortly after you make changes and are online.

Links and credentials

Add as many links as you like under Links & Social profiles when editing a card. Start typing a site (for example "lin" or "venmo") and Handleshare suggests the right address pattern; add your username at the end. Each link gets a name recipients see, and a preview image fetched by your phone from the page — tap a link to rename it, change its category, or choose or remove its image. Tap Edit to reorder, archive or delete links. A link whose page no longer exists gets a "Page not found" badge; nothing is archived or deleted for you.

License and ID numbers (NMLS, DRE, state licenses and so on) go under Credentials. In the share sheet every link and credential has its own switch, starting from its "Include in shares by default" setting. If the QR code gets dense, share fewer items or send a card link instead — it scans more reliably.

Attachments

You can attach files (PDFs, images, documents) to your card from Files, the camera, or your photo library, and choose which ones go out with each share or email. If the files you send add up to more than 20 MB, Handleshare sends a card link instead, so it arrives anywhere; AirDrop and Save to Files still get the files. You'll see a note before anything uploads.

Sharing a card as a link

In the share sheet, tap Create a link. Handleshare uploads the files you've ticked along with a contact card holding only the details you've switched on, and hands you a private web address on handleshare.me that anyone you send it to can open in a browser — no app needed on their end. Card pages aren't indexed by search engines, and their addresses can't be guessed. They see who it's from, can view your contact card and save it (photo included), and can download each file. If you've switched on your intro and bio, the page shows them too.

Free keeps 3 links live at a time, with files up to 10 MB each, for 7 days. Pro lifts the count, allows files up to 100 MB, lets you choose 7, 30 or 90 days or keep a link for as long as you have Pro (it ends about a week after Pro ends), extend a link that's already out, and update what a link shows after you've shared it, at the same address.

Managing or stopping a link

Swipe to the Card Links page, just before the scanner, or tap Links in the share sheet or Plan & Settings → Card Links. Every link you've made is listed with how many times it was opened. Tap Copy, Share, Extend, Rename or Expire — expiring deletes the uploaded files from iCloud, so the page dies immediately.

If a link has gone further than you meant, reset it: the same content moves to a new address, and the old address stops working at once. Share the new one with the people who should still have it. To change what a link shows without changing its address (Pro), open the card's Share sheet, choose what to include, then tap Links and Update.

Backing up and restoring

Open the profile switcher and tap Backups at the bottom, below Add New Profile. Back Up Now writes a single file holding every profile, attachment, scan and photo; share it to your Mac to keep a copy off the phone. To put one back, tap Restore next to it. Restoring is additive — profiles already on your iPhone are left alone, and anything missing is added back.

Backups also appear in Files under On My iPhone → Handleshare → Backups. Anyone who can open that file can read everything in it, so store it somewhere you trust.

Your plan, subscriptions and restoring a purchase

Open the profile switcher and tap Plan & Settings, below Add New Profile. It shows the plan you are on, how many profiles you have, and buttons to view the Pro plans, restore a previous purchase, and manage or cancel a subscription (which opens Apple's own subscription screen). Two profiles (with two presets each) are free forever; Pro is $2.99/month, $19.99/year, or $49 once, and lifts the profile, preset and card-link limits.

Anyone who downloaded HandleShare before version 3.0 keeps Pro free — there is nothing to buy or restore.

Deleting your data

Delete any profile from within the app to remove it from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes all local data; data in your private iCloud can be managed from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. To delete a card link's uploaded files, tap Expire on it in Card Links; otherwise they're deleted within a day of the link expiring.
