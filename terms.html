Handleshare is a digital business card app. It lets you create profile cards with contact information, links, Wi-Fi details, and file attachments, and share them via QR codes, the iOS share sheet, email, and similar mechanisms.

The app is free to use, and free includes two profile cards. Sharing, QR and Wi-Fi codes, the paper-card scanner, and file attachments are unlimited; card links have free-plan limits (section 5a). Handleshare Pro removes the two-card limit, raises the card-link limits, and is sold either as an auto-renewing subscription ($2.99 per month or $19.99 per year) or as a one-time purchase ($49). Prices are shown in your local currency at the point of purchase and may vary by region.

Subscriptions are billed to your Apple Account, renew automatically unless auto-renew is switched off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and can be managed or cancelled at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The one-time Lifetime purchase does not renew. Anyone who downloaded HandleShare before version 3.0 keeps Pro at no charge.

5a. Card links

A card link uploads a contact card with the details you've switched on to share, your note, and any files you select, to the app's iCloud database, and gives you a private, unguessable web address for them. Treat that address as the key: anyone who has it can open the page, save the contact card and download any files, so share it only with people you mean to.

Links expire on their own — 7 days on the free plan, or 7, 30 or 90 days with Pro — and you can stop one at any time from Plan & Settings → Card Links. The free plan allows 3 live links at a time and files up to 10 MB each; Pro raises both. Do not use card links to distribute anything unlawful, or anything you do not have the right to share. Every shared page carries a report link, and we may delete a reported or unlawful share without notice.
